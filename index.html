Tickets

Boleto Turístico

Buy the full Integral (S/130, valid 10 days) at the COSITUC office, Av. El Sol 103, open 7:30 AM–6:00 PM daily. Sold in person only and never sells out, so there is nothing to book ahead — but it is the only way into Ollantaytambo this afternoon and Sacsayhuamán on Sunday. Buy it here rather than at the gate: site kiosks often sell only their own circuit, and two partials cost more than the full ticket. Bring soles.
